### The Problem: The High Cost of Guest "Tricks" and Inefficient HVAC

The "card-in-slot" trick is legendary in the travel world. Guests leave business cards, old loyalty cards, or even pieces of folded paper in the power slot to keep the air conditioning blasting while they are at the pool or out for dinner. From the guest's perspective, it's a minor convenience. From the operator's perspective, it's a financial and environmental disaster.

Consider the challenges of current occupancy solutions:
- **Keycard Slots:** Easily tricked and prone to mechanical failure. Guests find them annoying and often feel "policed."
- **PIR Motion Sensors:** These are the most common alternative, but they are deeply flawed. PIR sensors require movement. If a guest is sleeping or sitting still watching a movie, the lights and AC might suddenly shut off. This leads to "the ghost in the room" complaints and guest frustration.
- **Door Switches:** These only tell you if a door was opened. They can't tell you if the guest left or if they just let a friend in. 
- **The Energy Drain:** HVAC accounts for roughly 40-50% of a hotel’s total energy consumption. Estimates suggest that 20-30% of that energy is wasted on rooms that are unoccupied. For a 200-room property, this waste can amount to tens of thousands of dollars annually.
- **Sustainability Pressure:** Investors and guests are increasingly demanding that hotels show real progress on ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) goals. Continuing to waste energy on empty rooms is no longer a viable option.

### The Solution: WifiSenseBox True Presence Intelligence

WifiSenseBox solves the occupancy puzzle once and for all. By using non-invasive WiFi-sensing technology, WifiSenseBox detects the "human signature" in a room with 99.9% accuracy. It doesn't look for motion; it looks for *life*.

WifiSenseBox analyzes the Channel State Information (CSI) of the WiFi signals already present in your building. Because the human body is mostly water, it affects WiFi signals in a very specific, detectable way. WifiSenseBox can detect the micro-movements of a sleeping guest's chest as they breathe, ensuring the AC stays on when they are in bed. Conversely, the moment they leave the room—regardless of whether they left a card in the slot—the system knows. This information is instantly communicated to your building management system (BMS), which can then set the HVAC to "eco-mode" or adjust the lighting.

### Technical Deep Dive: WiFi Sensing vs. The Competition

To appreciate the WifiSenseBox advantage, we must look at the science of occupancy detection.

**PIR (Passive Infrared):** Detects heat signatures moving across its field of view. 
*   *Failure Point:* Cannot detect a person who is stationary or behind an object (like a shower curtain or a bed headboard).

**Ultrasonic Sensors:** Send out high-frequency sound waves and listen for the reflection.
*   *Failure Point:* Can be triggered by moving air from the AC itself, leading to "false positives" where the AC stays on because the sensor thinks the moving air is a person.

**WiFi Sensing (CSI):**
- **Through-Obstacle Sensing:** WiFi signals pass through furniture, bedding, and even bathroom walls. WifiSenseBox provides 360-degree coverage of the entire room from a single, discreet device.
- **Biometric Sensitivity:** Our algorithms are tuned to detect the frequency of human respiration. This "life-sign" detection is the gold standard of occupancy sensing.
- **Zero-Maintenance:** No lenses to clean, no batteries to change (if hardwired), and no mechanical parts to wear out.
- **Edge Processing:** All signal analysis happens on the WifiSenseBox device. Your guest's presence data stays local and anonymous, only sending a simple "Occupied/Vacant" status to your BMS.
