Maintaining safety on a modern school or university campus is a balancing act. Administrators must prepare for the worst-case scenarios—fire, medical emergencies, or security lockdowns—while ensuring the campus remains an open, non-repressive environment for students and faculty.

The most critical information during an emergency is also the hardest to get: **How many people are left in the building, and exactly where are they?**

## The Problem: The High Stakes of Blind Evacuations

### The Challenges of Campus Safety:
*   **The Evacuation Blind Spot:** During a fire drill or real emergency, teachers and responders rely on manual roll calls, which are slow and prone to error in high-stress situations.
*   **The Privacy Barrier:** Kameras sind in Wohnheimen, Umkleideräumen und privaten Lernbereichen – genau den Orten, an denen Studenten am verletzlichsten sind – rechtlich und gesellschaftlich verboten.
*   **Asset Protection:** Libraries and labs contain millions of dollars in equipment, but constant video monitoring of students creates a culture of distrust.
