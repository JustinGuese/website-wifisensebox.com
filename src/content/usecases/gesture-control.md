## The Problem: The Interface Friction of Modern Homes

The way we interact with our smart devices today is filled with friction. We’ve replaced the physical simplicity of a wall switch with complex digital layers.

### Why Voice and Apps Fail for Quick Tasks:
*   **The "Social" Cost of Voice:** Voice commands are awkward when you have guests, disruptive when people are sleeping, and often require multiple attempts ("I'm sorry, I didn't catch that").
*   **The "App Fatigue" Trap:** Unlocking a phone, navigating to a folder, opening an app, and waiting for it to sync with the cloud just to dim a lamp is a step backward in usability.
*   **Privacy Concerns:** Voice assistants require "always-on" microphones that process your private conversations in the cloud.
*   **Physical Limitations:** If your hands are messy from cooking or wet from the shower, traditional touchscreens and physical switches are impractical.

We need a silent, private, and instant way to communicate with our environment.

## Technical Deep Dive: Mapping 3D Space with Radar

Capturing a gesture with radio waves is significantly more complex than doing so with a camera. Here’s how the WifiSenseBox engineering team made it possible.

### 1. Range-Doppler Maps
WifiSenseBox emits 60GHz radar pulses. When these pulses hit your hand, they return with a specific "time-of-flight" (distance) and a "Doppler shift" (velocity). We combine these into a Range-Doppler Map, which represents the hand's movement in real-time.

### 2. Micro-Gesture Recognition
Traditional radar can see a whole person moving. WifiSenseBox uses "high-resolution" radar that can distinguish individual fingers. This allows us to recognize complex "micro-gestures," like rubbing your thumb and forefinger together to mimic a volume dial.

### 3. Temporal Convolutional Networks (TCN)
A gesture isn't just a single point in time; it’s a sequence. WifiSenseBox uses a specialized type of AI called a Temporal Convolutional Network. This AI looks at the *history* of the movement over the last 500ms to determine if a "wave" was an intentional command or just you reaching for a glass of water.

### 4. Edge AI Processing
To ensure the system is responsive and private, we don't send your movement data to the cloud. All the complex math—the Fourier transforms and the neural network inferences—happens on the WifiSenseBox's internal processor.
