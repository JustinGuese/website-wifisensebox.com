## The Problem: The Privacy Paradox of Modern Home Security

The current home security market is dominated by "smart" cameras. While effective, they carry significant downsides that many homeowners are starting to reject.

### The Issues with Home Cameras:
*   **The "Always Watched" Feeling:** Living with cameras inside your home is psychologically taxing. It changes how you act and how you feel in your most private moments.

## Technical Deep Dive: The Science of Human Identification

How does a device "see" a human without a camera? It comes down to physics and pattern recognition.

### 1. Human RF Signature
The human body is mostly water, which makes it a very effective reflector and absorber of certain radio frequencies. As a person moves through a room, they create a unique "ripple" in the WiFi environment. WifiSenseBox tracks these ripples with millisecond precision.

### 2. Gait Analysis
Every person has a unique way of walking—a "gait." Even more broadly, humans walk differently than four-legged animals. WifiSenseBox uses Machine Learning models trained on thousands of hours of movement data to distinguish between the rhythmic, upright movement of a person and the low-to-the-ground, multi-limb movement of a pet.

### 3. Micro-Movement Validation
One of the hardest things for a security system is knowing the difference between a person and a large object (like a swinging door). WifiSenseBox solves this by looking for **life signs**. A person, even when trying to be still, has a heartbeat and a breathing rate. WifiSenseBox can detect these micro-movements to confirm that the "intruder" is indeed a biological entity.

### 4. Through-Obstacle Sensing
WifiSenseBox operates in the 2.4GHz to 60GHz range. Lower frequencies are excellent at passing through walls, while higher frequencies provide the resolution needed for precise tracking. By combining these, WifiSenseBox can monitor an entire apartment or a small house from a single central location.
