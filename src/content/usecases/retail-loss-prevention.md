## The Solution: WifiSenseBox Invisible Loss Prevention

WifiSenseBox doesn't "watch"—it "senses." By monitoring the electromagnetic environment of your store, it identifies human movement patterns that indicate a high probability of theft or unauthorized access.

### How WifiSenseBox Protects Your Bottom Line:

## Technical Deep Dive: Detecting the "Signature of Suspicion"

WifiSenseBox uses Machine Learning to classify movements in real-time.

### CSI Behavioral Mapping
Every human movement creates a unique "ripple" in the store's WiFi field. A person browsing comfortably has a distinct signal compared to someone attempting to conceal merchandise. Our AI isolates these signatures with high accuracy.
