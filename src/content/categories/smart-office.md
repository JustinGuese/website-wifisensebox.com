### The Technical Edge: WiFi Sensing vs. The Competition

To understand why WifiSenseBox is the superior choice for the modern office, one must look at the technical limitations of legacy systems. Traditional PIR sensors work by detecting heat signatures moving across their field of view. This means they require a direct line of sight. If an employee is behind a glass partition or even just a large monitor, the PIR sensor might fail to see them. Furthermore, PIR sensors are notoriously bad at detecting stationary people—leading to the all-too-common scenario of an employee having to wave their arms to turn the lights back on.

Camera-based systems, while accurate, are a privacy nightmare. Even with on-device processing ("edge AI"), the mere presence of a lens creates a culture of surveillance that many employees find toxic. Acoustic sensors can be triggered by external noise, and Bluetooth/WiFi tracking requires employees to carry devices and have their radios turned on, which is unreliable and invasive.

WifiSenseBox uses Channel State Information (CSI) from WiFi signals. As WiFi waves travel through a room, they bounce off objects and people. Human movement, even the subtle movement of a chest while breathing, creates a unique signature in the CSI data. WifiSenseBox's proprietary algorithms analyze these signatures in real-time to provide high-fidelity occupancy data. Because WiFi signals penetrate common building materials, a single WifiSenseBox can often cover multiple rooms or large open-plan areas, reducing the total hardware cost (TCO) compared to installing dozens of PIR sensors.
