## Beyond Senior Care: Clinical Intelligence

WifiSenseBox transforms healthcare environments by providing high-fidelity patient data where cameras are forbidden. From ICU monitoring to post-op recovery, our technology ensures that no patient is ever truly alone, even in private rooms and bathrooms.

### Why Healthcare Leaders Choose WifiSenseBox:
- **Zero-Visual Monitoring:** Respects HIPAA and patient privacy while providing 24/7 visibility.
- **Micro-Doppler Vitals:** Tracks respiratory rates and micro-movements through bedding.
- **Infection Control:** Contactless sensing means no cross-contamination between patients.
- **Staff Efficiency:** Reduces the need for manual "wellness checks," allowing nurses to focus on high-acuity tasks.
