## Privacy: The Foundation, Not an Afterthought

In the race to make homes "smarter," many companies have treated privacy as a secondary concern—or worse, as a commodity to be traded for features. We’ve been told that if we want the convenience of a smart home, we must accept the surveillance of cameras and the eavesdropping of microphones.

**We reject that trade-off.**

Privacy is not a "feature" of WifiSenseBox; it is the core of our architectural philosophy. By using radio waves instead of light (cameras) or sound (microphones), we have built a system that is physically incapable of violating your visual or auditory privacy. 

### Why RF-Sensing is the Privacy Standard:
*   **No Image Data:** Radar sees "clouds" of movement, not faces or bodies. It cannot distinguish between your clothing or your features.
*   **Local Processing:** We don't believe your home's movement data belongs in the cloud. Every WifiSenseBox unit is a powerful edge-computer that processes all sensing data locally.
*   **GDPR Compliance by Design:** Because we don't collect "Personally Identifiable Information" (PII) through our sensors, we meet the highest global standards for data protection from the ground up.

## Energy Efficiency: The Green Smart Home

A home that understands you is also a home that respects the planet. One of the biggest wastes of energy in modern homes is heating, cooling, and lighting empty rooms. Because traditional sensors are unreliable, we often set long "time-outs" (e.g., waiting 20 minutes to turn off the AC after someone leaves).

With WifiSenseBox’s precision, you can reduce those time-outs to seconds. The moment a room is truly empty, WifiSenseBox knows. It can immediately signal your HVAC and lighting systems to enter energy-saving modes. For a typical household, this can lead to a 20-30% reduction in energy consumption, paying for the device itself in just a few months.
