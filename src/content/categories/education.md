## Protecting the Future: Smart Campus Intelligence

Educational institutions face the unique challenge of providing high-level security while maintaining an open, welcoming, and privacy-respecting environment. WifiSenseBox provides the data educators need to ensure campus safety, optimize facility use, and protect student well-being without a single camera.

### Why Educational Leaders Choose WifiSenseBox:
- **Evacuation Management:** Real-time, non-visual headcount during emergencies or drills.
- **Vandalism Prevention:** Detects unauthorized presence in libraries, labs, and offices after hours.
- **Resource Optimization:** Understand which study spaces and collaborative zones are actually used to guide future investments.
- **Privacy-First Security:** High-level safety in sensitive areas like dormitories and locker rooms where cameras are legally prohibited.
